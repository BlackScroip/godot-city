### Flying-dragon trails

The six serialized Boss 53001 trail descriptors have been connected to Godot trail geometry as part of the ongoing embedded-effect implementation. They all reference material `CAB-e355e6b0d05ab47d2012d0e8ccc2b413:-7520495672365228611` (`mat_fx_24122501_trail_trail`), with source duration 0.2 seconds, minimum vertex distance 0.25 and `_FadeAmount` 0.25. Source prefab bundle SHA-256 is `79a1fe9e3c8cfafcb6c5b373902fc6f87c8133c804e5ddd3a4d435677a4823f0`; material bundle SHA-256 is `653524e8999548ee14286f10eca067bad27dcb3e41dbc46d25b1f50c5fa39a50`.

That material's exact keyword set is `FADE_ON`, `SHAPE1ROTATE_ON`, `SHAPE1SHAPECOLOR_ON`. Its referenced shader bundle SHA-256 is `c1dba3b6790f844eb1123b5a9e958e80a1074c5cd1f09dc7127fe4153bb7f3a7`; GLES platform 9 program 531 has SHA-256 `c9f346ad24a3c29072c97295fab6d74e642a42521766a200ee7844dfca7066a7`. Its vertex stage passes vertex color through, and its fragment fade input is `clamp(1 - vertex_alpha + _FadeAmount, 0, 1)`. The output alpha is main-texture red times fade times `_Alpha`, without another vertex-alpha multiplier. This formula is evidence for this exact serialized program and keyword set only; it is not established for other variants or graphics backends.

The project-side geometry and shader behavior are not an observation of the original APK at runtime. Unity's trail vertex generation, movement, camera, blending and final appearance still need a device-side comparison; the extracted shader alone does not establish visual parity.

## Flower Boss Star

The `26071801_fx_enemy_mojiehua_attack3_duyejidang` prefab's `26070901_shoubuxuli_xingxing` particle renderer references material `CAB-e355e6b0d05ab47d2012d0e8ccc2b413:6028709936780744974`. Its shader pointer is `CAB-e0e868e1a4508c641226989c4c20c91b:-7807925893294491066` (`AllIn1Vfx/AllIn1VfxSRPBatch`). The shader's sole pass binds both RGB and alpha blend factors to `_SrcMode` and `_DstMode`, which the material serializes as 1 and 1 (One/One). Its separately serialized `_SrcBlend` and `_DstBlend` are 1 and 0, but that pair is not bound by this pass. The `Flare7` main texture, decoded directly from the APK, is 512 by 512 with alpha 255 throughout; its corner pixel is black and its center pixel is white.

The exact-keyword GLES combined program 1063 (`ADDITIVECONFIG_ON`, `SHAPE1ROTATE_ON`, `VERTOFFSET_ON`; SHA-256 `dc1f92f8434187f565fef0606a2e2116e60336ce27d0c8b086594812ff0d8efc`) samples RGBA and calculates `A = texture.a * _ShapeColor.a * vertex_color.a * _Alpha`; RGB is `texture.rgb * _ShapeColor.rgb * vertex_color.rgb * _Color.rgb * A`, and output alpha is `A`. Thus the black texture corners contribute no RGB under One/One blending. The program entry is stored in the pass's `progVertex` table and contains both vertex and fragment sections; this does not imply a separate `progFragment` table entry.

For 2,250 exported descriptors from the same material file naming this shader, source shader pointers and blend properties were checked against the APK. Of those, 89 have `_SrcMode/_DstMode` 1/1 while the `_SrcBlend/_DstBlend` pair differs; the pre-fix descriptor classifications were 79 add, 3 alpha and 7 unknown. Nineteen same-name descriptors in other serialized files were not pointer-verified; none of their exported `_SrcMode/_DstMode` pairs is 1/1. This establishes the shared pass binding, not the exact fragment calculation for other keyword combinations. APK runtime blending, color-space conversion and final device appearance were not observed.

## Implementation and Runtime Checks

- Projectile creation now retains authored warnings when a model is also specified. Concurrent wolf warnings are prewarmed, and important warnings can displace ordinary effects under budget pressure. Selected flower poison, earth-dragon rockfall, and flying-dragon lightning effects preserve more of their source burst schedule; this does not establish coverage for every runtime path.
- The flower poison effect keeps its source lifetime and is protected against eviction by ordinary hit effects while still being recycled on action changes. Its low-quality template allows 15 emitters, retaining the main charge, rings, trails, splashes and selected smoke layers. The overall low-quality cap remains 48 emitters and 768 particles; full quality remains 128 and 2,048. Lower density settings impose smaller limits on ordinary effects, while short-lived Boss priority effects may use headroom up to those hard caps. For long-lived Boss priority effects at reduced density, 23 low-quality or 36 full-quality emitter slots are reserved for transient effects. Maximum-density long-lived allocation retains its previous capacity. An impossible allocation is rejected before evicting otherwise eligible effects.
- Four default Reaper prefab effect roots and the flying dragon's 24 phase-specific particle roots and six wing trails are connected. Trail geometry has separate point caps of 24 at full quality and 16 at low quality. Recovery after budget pressure is queued and rechecks available capacity. A headless mixed-queue regression (`tests/boss_trail_performance_test.gd -- --functional-only`) passed 38 checks across both quality settings, including recovery alongside five ordinary roots without duplicate spawns or budget overruns.
- Desktop Godot rendered tests passed for wolf `attack2-normal` and `attack3-gated`, flower `attack3` at both qualities, the Reaper and dragon embedded effects, and the isolated flower and trail shader blend regressions. In the flower low-quality fixed-step test, frame 235 retained the poison effect alongside five visible hit roots at every density. Peak emitters/particles were 43/151 at minimum density and 46/213 at maximum density. Pixel-difference checks establish visibility in the test scene, not visual equivalence or player readability in every encounter. Reproduce with `Godot --path goDot --script res://tests/flower_poison_visual_test.gd -- --quality=low --density=0` using a graphical renderer; density ranges from 0 to 2, and quality can also be `full`.
- Headless checks included 50,978 assertions across 1,085 imported effect definitions, 658 layer-budget checks, 65 reservation-pressure checks, 1,257 source-presentation checks, and 100 summon-effect checks. The reservation test uses the flower Boss's real poison-pool summon to stress both reduced-density settings and checks rejected allocations against effect, particle, emitter and pool constraints. On this desktop, the summon transform-update microbenchmark measured 18,000 updates for 60 summons at 51.462 ms before indexing and 9.633 ms after; it excludes rendering, GPU work and device frame time. Restored visual layers and priority effects can increase other costs, so this result does not establish that every boss encounter is faster.

## Coverage limits

The action-reference inventory does not audit embedded prefab effects, animation events, preload-only assets or all possible action fields; the separate prefab-object inventory is also bounded as described above. Descriptor and checked path existence do not establish correct material conversion, timing, positioning, reachability, on-screen visibility or visual equivalence to the APK. At maximum density, an extreme number of protected long-lived effects can still fill the hard budget and block later feedback; lowering density during that saturation does not immediately rebalance existing instances. The targeted desktop tests above do not cover every action, original APK runtime rendering or mobile-device CPU/GPU performance. Neither static inventory establishes CPU improvement or APK gameplay parity.
